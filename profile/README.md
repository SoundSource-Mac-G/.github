## What Is SoundSource for Mac

<a href="#">soundsource mac</a> is a per-application audio control utility for macOS developed by Rogue
Amoeba, a company known for its professional Mac audio software. It sits in your menu bar and gives you
fast access to your Mac's most important audio settings — the input, output and sound-effects devices,
the volume for each of them, and the volume of every individual application that is making sound.

macOS on its own offers only a single system volume and one output device at a time. <a href="#">audio
control mac</a> with SoundSource replaces that limited model with a full mixing panel: each running app
gets its own slider, its own output device and its own set of effects. If you want your music quiet in
one app while a video plays loud in another, or you want a call routed to your headphones while system
sounds stay on the speakers, SoundSource makes it a two-click adjustment instead of an impossibility.

## Per-Application Volume and Routing

The core of <a href="#">download soundsource for mac</a> is <a href="#">per app audio mac</a> control.
Every application that plays sound appears in the panel with independent controls.

Because <a href="#">soundsource mac download</a> works on a per-application basis, you are no longer stuck
